# Executable Atlas revision, 12 October 2026

This revision repairs code, data interpretation and reproducibility infrastructure. It does not revise the manuscript or Overleaf project.

Raw snapshots and historical imported code remain intact. Revised builders live in `code/reconstruction`; original/imported identities are recorded in `metadata/reconstruction_source_imports.csv`. Input and output manifests are separate. Historical audit failures are evidence of the old version and are not rewritten as passes.

## Reproducibility repairs

- Native/application modules are present. Replay uses an explicit `ORACLE_WORKSPACE`, fresh outputs and bounded DuckDB settings.
- Python and direct scientific dependencies are pinned. Bootstrap records logs, package versions and host/scientific identities.
- The Pyth snapshot is archived with its checksum. Replay reproduces 49 epochs, 6,566 factors and 134 counters; full replay compares all rows with reference tables.
- Decision experiments use an explicit feature allowlist and train-only preprocessing. Outcome-derived actor roles and observed cross-chain links are excluded from financial T0 features. Retrospective OATA trajectories are not certified decision-time inputs.
- Croissant metadata for the original review subset has canonical JSON-LD context, Boolean types, immutable URLs, RAI descriptions and explicit provenance limits. All 15 record sets are read with `mlcroissant==1.1.0`; format success does not prove complete historical human provenance.
- The tutorial saves two deterministic case runs, logs, host/scientific freezes and a ZIP. Local notebook execution is distinguished from hosted Google Colab execution.

## Remaining scope boundaries

The historical v1.5 audit had two mismatches among 1,395 registered hashes. Expected and observed digests remain in `reports/revision_20261012/historical_hash_mismatches.json`; this revision does not invent their cause or claim recovery of those historical bytes. New files receive new immutable identities.

Timestamp gaps, right censoring and protocol-specific observability remain in the data. The field dictionary explains these conditions rather than fabricating values. Some native-field descriptions are type/source-reference documentation, explicitly distinguished from reviewed common/episode semantics.

Hosted Colab execution, a repository owner's review/merge, a dataset DOI and complete historical acquisition/person-agent provenance need separate evidence. They are not inferred from a local build. Historical OATA model scripts and unimplemented survival families are not advertised as validated experiments.
